# 💣 Bomb's Away!

## 🎯 Mission Synopsis

Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to hit the target!

**Bomb's Away!** is a unique skill-based challenge that tests your timing, judgment, and understanding of physics. You'll take on the role of a "Bombardier," tasked with accurately dropping an inert payload from a custom-built, remotely piloted aircraft onto a designated ground target.

Our experienced pilots will handle the complexities of flight, but **you** hold the crucial control: a single trigger for payload release. The objective is simple, but the challenge is immense! Calculate the perfect moment to release, accounting for aircraft speed, altitude, and wind to land as close to the target's center as possible.

---

## 🏆 Objective

The goal is to release an inert payload from the RC aircraft and have it land as close as possible to the center of a large ground target marked with an 'X'. **The most accurate drop wins!**

---

## 📜 Gameplay & Rules

* **Participation**: This is a solo event.
* **Attempts**: Each participant gets **three** attempts to drop the payload.
* **Scoring**: After each drop, a marshal will measure the distance from the target's center to the payload's point of impact. Your final score will be the **single closest distance** out of your three attempts.
* **Tie-Breaker**: In case of a tie, participants will engage in a single "sudden death" drop to determine the winner.
